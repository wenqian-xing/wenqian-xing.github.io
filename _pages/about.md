I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student in [Operations Research](https://or.stanford.edu/) at Stanford University, advised by [Ramesh Johari](https://web.stanford.edu/~rjohari/). I also work closely with [Yue Hu](https://gsb-faculty.stanford.edu/yue-hu/), [Tianyi Peng](https://tianyipeng.github.io/), and [Woody Zhu](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/shixianz/).  

I received my M.S. in Operations Research from Columbia University and a B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from UM-SJTU Joint Institute at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I was advised by [Hong Chen](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=LAGGFe0AAAAJ&hl=en), [Cheng Hua](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=NWCZzR8AAAAJ&hl=en), [Henry Lam](https://www.columbia.edu/~khl2114/), and [David X. Li](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=oQSIrMkAAAAJ&hl=en). I have also been a research intern at Amazon Core AI and Microsoft Research, and completed my bachelor's thesis at Intel.  

My research interests broadly lie in stochastic modeling and decision-making under uncertainty, with a focus on causal inference, reinforcement learning, and AI evaluation and alignment.
