I’m a forth-year Ph.D. student in [Operations Research](https://or.stanford.edu/) at Stanford University, where I am advised by [Ramesh Johari](https://web.stanford.edu/~rjohari/), and to work closely with [Yue Hu](https://gsb-faculty.stanford.edu/yue-hu/), [Tianyi Peng](https://tianyipeng.github.io/), and [Woody Zhu](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/shixianz/). I received an M.S. in Operations Research from Columbia University and a B.S.E. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Michigan–Shanghai Jiao Tong University Joint Institute, where I was advised by [Henry Lam](https://www.engineering.columbia.edu/faculty-staff/directory/henry-lam), [Hong Chen](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=LAGGFe0AAAAJ&hl=en), and [Cheng Hua](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=NWCZzR8AAAAJ&hl=en). I have also worked as a research intern at Amazon Core AI and Microsoft Research.

My research interests lie broadly in stochastic modeling and decision-making under uncertainty, with a focus on causal inference, reinforcement learning, and AI evaluation and alignment.
