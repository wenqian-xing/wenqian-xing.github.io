I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student in Operations Research at Stanford University, advised by [Ramesh Johari](https://web.stanford.edu/~rjohari/). I also work closely with [Yue Hu](https://gsb-faculty.stanford.edu/yue-hu/), [Tianyi Peng](https://tianyipeng.github.io/), and [Woody Zhu](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/shixianz/). 

I received my M.S. in Operations Research from Columbia University and my B.S.E. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I have also been a research intern at Amazon Core AI and Microsoft Research.  

My research interests broadly lie in stochastic modeling and decision-making under uncertainty, with a focus on causal inference, reinforcement learning, and AI evaluation and alignment.
